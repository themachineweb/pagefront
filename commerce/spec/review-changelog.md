### 0.1.0 — 2026-11-17

Released in commerce release 0.6.0, the first release that bundles this sheet spec; ratified from the draft of the same day.

- **New sheet type (draft).** The Commerce Review Sheet, 0.1.0, drafted 2026-11-17: an `ItemList`-rooted sheet carrying the consumer reviews a publisher shows for one subject, a product, a variant family or the seller, named in `about`. A sheet-level `pagefront:reviewPolicy` states how reviews reach the set; every entry is a Schema.org `Review` carrying four declarations, `pagefront:reviewSubject`, `pagefront:reviewSource`, `pagefront:purchaseVerification` and `pagefront:reviewIncentive`, each a closed enum with an `undisclosed` member, and optionally the aspects it addresses in `pagefront:aspect` with `pagefront:aspectSource`. The list is exhaustive, in `datePublished` order, originals only. Reviews attach to the `ProductGroup` where one exists. The subject's sheet points at it through `subjectOf`; the page declares it beside the subject's sheet under "Several Sheets for one page" in Pagefront Discovery 0.2.0, which this spec requires. Linter codes REV-E-001 to REV-E-012 and REV-W-001 to REV-W-011 are reserved; the product-side and organization-side checks await a multi-sheet linter. The editable schema head is [`review.json`](review.json); no schema is published until the release that bundles the spec.
- **Change (draft): `unclassified` subject.** `pagefront:reviewSubject` gains `unclassified`: the publisher has not determined the subject, a different unknown from `undisclosed`, since the text is in the publisher's hands and the fact is knowable; a consumer treats it as `mixed` for every aggregate, so it enters no product aggregate. A producer MUST NOT emit it on a classified review or beside `pagefront:aspect` entries (REV-W-012, new). "Undisclosed is a statement" scopes its first sentence to the provenance declarations and the policy, adds the `reviewSubject` states and the minimum entry; "Scoping the aggregate" excludes `unclassified`; REV-E-012 also fires on `unclassified` on an Organization review sheet. The worked example's third review becomes the minimum entry. **(schema)** the enum gains the value.
- **Change (draft): `pagefront:ReviewSource` minted; `pagefront:incentive` renamed.** `pagefront:reviewSource` is one `pagefront:ReviewSource`, with `@type`, as every object-valued `pagefront:` property carries a typed value; the worked example carries it. `pagefront:incentive` becomes `pagefront:reviewIncentive`, since a generic word in a flat namespace is one promotions or loyalty will want and an IRI cannot be renamed once published. **(schema)** `@type` required on the source; the property renamed. **(linter)** REV-E-008 reads the new name.
- **Reconciled with the vocabulary.** The status line names commerce release 0.6.0; "Vocabulary additions" says the terms are in `vocabulary.md`; the host rule on the `subjectOf` and `about` edges is on the URL alone, with the `@id` confirmed by exact string; the solicitation and source enums are bulleted with their meanings, which `vocabulary.md` mirrors verbatim; the `author` bullet no longer points anonymity at `pagefront:reviewSource`. REV-E-013 added: a policy or source without its `@type`. **(linter)** REV-E-013 implemented.
- **Required means error; envelope rules.** `pagefront:aspectSource` beside `pagefront:aspect` and `bestRating`/`worstRating` on a `ratingValue` above 5 are schema requirements, checked by REV-E-014 and REV-E-015, the two warnings first written for them withdrawn. `url` on a curated policy, `name` on a platform source and `inLanguage` beside text become SHOULD, their warnings unchanged. REV-E-016 and REV-E-017 mirror COL-E-003 and COL-E-004. The set `@id` is the page URL with `#reviews` and `about.@id` the subject's declared `@id`; the example platform is fictional; the declaration headings and the contrast forms follow the house style; inline `review[]` is a SHOULD NOT; the Discovery dependency names the feature rather than a version. PDP-W-044 fires on `ratingCount` above `numberOfItems`.
- **Design notes.** The rationale for the undisclosed state, the subject split, the variant family as subject, the exhaustive list, originals only and the closed aspect list moves to [`review-notes.md`](review-notes.md), non-normative, each note linked from the section it explains with a `why` link; the reference keeps every requirement. The notes carry this document's version line and log their changes here.
